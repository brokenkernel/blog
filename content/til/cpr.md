The success rate of <abbr title="Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation">CPR</abbr> is quite complicated. Nonetheless we have some interesting facts.

1. Overall survival from <abbr title="out-of-hospital cardiac arrest">OHCA</abbr> has been stable for almost 30 years
1. The aggregate survival rate, is between 6.7% and 8.4%
1. 5% of patients resuscitated with conventional CPR and more than 20% of those resuscitated with extracorporeal CPR were diagnosed with brain death
1. OHCA victims who receive CPR from a bystander or an <abbr title="Emergency Medical Services">EMS</abbr> provider are much more likely to survive than those who do not
1. The most powerful criterion associated with survival from OHCA is <abbr title="Return of spontaneous circulation">ROSC</abbr> in the field. Failure to restore a pulse on scene indicates that the patient will not likely survive to hospital discharge,  irrespective of the subsequent sophistication of in-hospital care
